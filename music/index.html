          Library & playback

          Choose your Music folder. Audio files are the library; optional musicdump.csv adds metadata, and M3Us add playlists. Refresh rereads your files. Nothing is uploaded. Only actual track starts append three fields to music-history.tsv: local timestamp, filename, music-tool.

          Find & listen

          Try rahman 199 spb. Every word matches metadata or a file path; initials and small typos work too. Searching selects the first result. Press Enter in search to focus it, then use Up/Down to select a song and Enter to play. Click column headings to sort. Double-click anywhere on a song row or press Enter to play it now. It is inserted directly after the current Queue item, preserving later songs. Right-click a library value to filter it, Play now, Play next, Play at end, Play 10 similar next or Info. Queue’s Locate in playlist clears search and reveals the song, switching to All tracks if needed. Drag Queue songs to reorder, or right-click for Add 10 similar here to insert after the selected song, and moves and removals, including all above/below. Played songs remain visible, and the current item is highlighted. Removing the current item or clearing the Queue stops playback. Shuffle queue rearranges songs after the current item. Repeat all cycles the entire Queue. Natural completion at the end stops playback without adding songs. Pressing Play or Next at the end appends up to ten available songs similar to the latest Queue song and starts the first. If none qualify, playback pauses with a message. Play Any appends ten random songs from the current playlist/search and starts that batch; small results repeat songs to reach ten.

          The Queue is synced to queue.tsv in your Music folder: two columns, path and status (played/current/next). It restores paused, without a new history entry. Refresh also rereads this file. Songs opened outside the folder remain in this session only. A gentler volume curve gives balanced control throughout the range; new users start at 100%. Mute keeps the selected level. Resume points stay in memory for up to 24 hours; replaying continues where you stopped, unless fewer than 15 seconds remained. Previous’s restart and repeat-one start at zero. Play after a song finishes continues the Queue, adding similar songs at its end, instead of replaying the finished song.

          Play 10 similar next uses CSV genre, composer, singer, year and album scores from play-music, choosing from the top 30 with slight randomness and penalties for repeated albums and people. It adds up to ten after the current song without interrupting playback; Queue’s Add 10 similar here adds after the selected song instead. All similar-song selections exclude songs played in the last seven days or 200 listening entries, whichever is less: only entries within both limits are excluded, using your history file and this session. No playlist preference bonuses are inferred; songs without matching metadata may have no similar results.

          Open files from Ubuntu

          After deployment at tools.s-anand.net/music/, install Music with the browser’s Install app command (or the Install button here). Visiting alone does not register file handlers. Allow file handling if prompted, then select Music in Ubuntu’s Open With / default application for .mp3, .m3u, and .m3u8. An MP3 opens directly; a playlist needs access to its tracks via your selected Music folder. Reconnect if permission expires.

          Offline listening

          Before your flight, open Music online, choose your Music folder, and wait for “Offline ready”. Only the app is cached; songs stay in your Music folder. You can then reopen the same URL in the same browser profile in flight mode; Reconnect can renew folder permission offline. Installing the app is optional. Clearing site data removes the cached player. Updates offer Refresh or Later and leave playback running until you choose Refresh.

          Script & agent control

          Use window.music, or dispatch music-control on window with the command in detail. music-state emits compact state after changes. Folder picking and permission requests require a genuine user click; scripts cannot grant folder access. 

          music.control({action: 'forward', seconds: 5})
window.dispatchEvent(new CustomEvent('music-control',
  {detail: {action: 'pause'}}))
music.playM3U('New.m3u', {shuffle: true, repeat: 'all'})
// Also: {action:'play-m3u', path:'New.m3u', shuffle:true, repeat:'all'}
music.find('rahman 199 spb'); music.getTrack(id)
music.findSimilar(id); music.similarity(id, otherId)
music.control({action: 'play-similar', track: id})
music.control({action: 'locate-track', track: id})
music.getState(); music.getQueue(); music.getHistory({limit:20})

          Actions: play, pause, toggle, forward/backward (seconds), seek (seconds), next/previous, speed/volume/repeat (value), play-track/queue-track/play-next/show-info (track: id), remove-queue/play-queue/remove-above/remove-below (index), move-queue (index, to: index or up/down/top/bottom), clear-queue, shuffle-queue, shuffle-visible, play-any, mute, volume-up/volume-down, filter (query), select (id), clear-search, focus-search, focus-results, focus-playlists, play-similar/locate-track (track: id or queue index), play-m3u (path, shuffle=false, repeat='off'). Volume 0–1 is perceptual; speed uses browser playbackRate. Unsupported speeds or codecs show an error.
