Help

Sometimes, it’s hard to figure out what to do next.

One classic approach is to list all your items, rate them on an urgency and importance scale, and pick those on the top right quadrant. Stephen Covey explains this in his book First Things First.

This can be generalised. Almost anything can be prioritised on a grid.

This page is a live multi-user single-page app that lets you add things and move them around. You can pick your axis scales. Everything is cached in your browser for offline use and synchronized through Cloud Firestore when connected.

Usage

  • Add an item by clicking on the “New item” button on top. You can edit and move the item around.
  • Delete the item by delete the text in it. It will vanish in 5 seconds after it loses focus. (If you change your mind, click on the item and press Ctrl-Z to undo.)
  • Change the grid: Use + to add rows or columns; clear a label to remove it.
  • Change item colors: Ctrl-click an item to change its color.
  • Your actions are automatically saved locally and synchronized to the room in the URL. Anyone possessing the complete link can edit.
  • Change rooms by selecting a locally cached room in the dropdown, or use New room for a clean shared grid.
  • Share the complete URL or QR code to collaborate live. Use Delete room to remove its Firestore document.
Coordinates may be percentages or 0–1 fractions; Y is measured from the bottom.
