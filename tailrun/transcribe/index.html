Transcribe audio

Choose an audio or video file from your phone or computer. Tailrun converts it to a small Opus file and uses your call script to write a Markdown transcript.

The Opus and Markdown files are saved to ~/Dropbox/COPY/transcribe/ on your runner. You can open or download them here.

Leave blank to use the current date and time. Do not include .opus or .md.

Adds context to the normal call prompt. Does not replace it.
