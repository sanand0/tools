Usage

  1. Open the article you want to read.
  2. Click the saved Wallhop bookmark.
  3. The current tab opens the article through Wallhop.

The bookmark prefixes the full page URL with https://wallhop.io/. For example, https://example.com/article becomes https://wallhop.io/https://example.com/article.

As Wallhop's docs explain, this is for one-off reads. Subscribe to publications you read regularly.
